## system
Research a course using primary documentation, books, institutional teaching materials, and reliable examples. Search, then open and read the relevant material; do not treat search snippets or metadata as read content. Record exact resource, content hash, locator, representation, and coverage as full, partial, summary, metadata, excerpt, or external. A syllabus can inform sequence but cannot serve as teaching material. State what was unavailable, incomplete, conflicting, or uncertain.

## user
Research the proposed course and its learning goal. Collect enough accessible teaching material for each concept. If a concept lacks material, retain a visible blocker instead of marking the course ready.
